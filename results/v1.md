# Verified v1 Result

On 2026-09-10, the bounded Coins + GSR bridge completed a proof-authorized
lifecycle on a clean GSR regtest: two deposits were collected, a genuine Coins
transfer and withdrawal were settled, and two later deposits were collected
against the settlement successor. An independently signed deposit refund also
succeeded. No bridge signing key or verifier stub was present in these accepted
bridge spends.

The two collection times are not benchmark-comparable: collection 1 was proved

each on the configured research node (`-acceptnonstdtxn=1`). All three spends
were then accepted in blocks. These timings include full transaction validation;
they are a single-run observation, not a benchmark distribution.

The 76,400-byte verifier is identical for every proof and has SHA256
`bf8b1e8f02a2f0c4f2ac251080d206e405d550a06e03805a140b9a67c4647df5`.
The reported varops number is the independently replayed arithmetic verifier
fragment. Core separately accepted the complete transaction under the exact
weight-derived budget; no instrumentation currently reports the exact total
varops consumed by the full multi-input transaction.

## Enforcement demonstrated

- OP_TX authenticates transaction shape, lineage, amounts, scripts, successor,
  caboose state, and exact proof statement.
- OP_TWEAKADD authenticates complete identity-bound deposit programs; normal
  BIP340 `OP_CHECKSIG` authorizes immediate refunds.
- Restored concatenation, slicing, bitwise, and arithmetic opcodes build and bind
  the statement. The Groth16 verifier uses GSR arithmetic macros, not a native
  pairing or field opcode.
- The guest checks fixed account roots, native and bridge-scoped Coins
  signatures, nonces, balance arithmetic, deposit credits, and withdrawal debit.
- Mutated proofs/statements, deposit substitution, partial forwarding, altered
  payouts/destinations, extra outputs, stale proofs, and caboose mismatch failed.
  Invalidate/reconsider tests restored the correct bridge head.

## Limits

This proves the bounded three-account relation, not a production L2. It adds no
privacy, general batching, unilateral exit for already-collected balances, or
data-availability guarantee. The proof system, experimental GSR consensus rules,
deployment setup, state publication, proving resources, fees, and inclusion
remain assumptions. The embedded account and refund secrets are public regtest
fixtures. Source redistribution terms noted in `sources.lock.json` remain to be
resolved before publication.
